About TRND Station

We supply retailers with international packaged snacks, sweets, drinks, squishy toys and select novelty items by the case. Our focus is simple: interesting products, a clear way to connect, and room to keep finding what comes next.

Questions retailers ask

Who we sell to

We supply retailers and businesses buying products for resale. Contact us to discuss your store and product needs.

Minimum order

Our minimum order is $200.

Free shipping

Yes. Free shipping is available within the US on orders over $500. For other orders, or for delivery times, contact us for shipping details.

Products we carry

International packaged chips, savory snacks, sweets, drinks, squishy toys and select novelty products. Toys are separate from our food assortment.
